Be my neighbor in Mindscape

Mindscape locks the apps that steal your time. Skip them, finish a focus session, and you earn coins to build a little village. Add a friend, and they move in as your neighbor.

To open Mindscape:

  1. Tap ⋯ at the top of the screen
  2. Choose Open in browser

Mindscape opens straight on your friend's invite. No app yet? You'll land in the App Store.
