Mindscape locks the apps that steal your time and gives you a few deliberate ways back in. Skip a ticket, respect a cooldown or finish a focus session, and you earn coins to grow your village.

How tickets work

  • You get 3 tickets a day. A ticket unlocks your chosen apps for 20 minutes.
  • Hold the button to start a ticket. After a ticket ends there is a 60-minute cooldown before the next one.
  • Tickets reset every night at 04:00.
  • Every ticket you don't use earns coins when the day ends.

What if I really need an app?

When you have no ticket available, you can use the emergency unlock once a day. Hold the button for 60 seconds, and your apps open for 10 minutes. It isn't available during a focus session.

Focus sessions

Pick a length from 5 to 120 minutes and start the timer. You earn coins for every focused minute. With deep focus on, every app is locked until the timer ends, except iPhone essentials like Phone, Messages and Maps, and any apps you choose to keep open (music, say).

Changing apps or turning Mindscape off

Adding apps locks them right away. Removing apps, or turning Mindscape off, takes 24 hours, and you can cancel any time before then. This delay is on purpose: it stops a weak moment from undoing your plan. Within the first hour after you turn Mindscape on or add apps, changes are instant, so a wrong pick isn't punished.

You'll find both in the app's Screen time tab.

Neighbors

Share your neighbor code or QR code with a friend. When they add it, your character moves into their village and wanders around. Nothing is sent through a server: the code goes only where you share it.

Common questions

Does Mindscape see what I do in my apps?
No. Apple's Screen Time gives the app anonymous tokens for the apps you pick, never their names or what you do in them. Read more in the privacy policy.

Screen Time says it isn't available.
Screen Time needs a real iPhone signed into iCloud. Check that Screen Time is turned on in iOS Settings, and that you allowed Mindscape when it asked.

Can I buy coins?
No. Coins can only be earned, never bought.

Do I need an account?
No. There are no accounts, and everything is stored on your iPhone.
